# ☁️ Multi-Cloud FinOps Platform

<p align="center">

<img src="assets/cloud-preview.png" width="100%"/>

</p>

### *Because cloud bills shouldn't require a detective.*

Managing three cloud providers shouldn't mean opening fifteen browser tabs.

So I built a centralized platform that helps businesses understand **where their cloud budget goes**, **why it goes there**, and **how to spend less without sacrificing performance**.

Instead of bouncing between AWS, Azure and GCP dashboards...

everything lives in one place.

Simple.

Fast.

Actually useful.

---

## ✨ Highlights

- ☁️ Unified Multi-Cloud Dashboard
- 📊 Cost Analytics & Visualization
- 🤖 AI-powered Cost Optimization
- 📈 Spending Forecasts
- 🚨 Intelligent Anomaly Detection
- ⚙️ Smart Scaling Recommendations
- 🔍 Resource Utilization Insights

---

## 🧠 The Hardest Challenge

Normalizing billing data from three different cloud providers into one consistent format without losing meaningful insights.

---

## 💡 What I Learned

> Great dashboards don't just show data.

> They tell users **what matters** and **what to do next.**

# 💰 Velora

<p align="center">

<img src="assets/velora-preview.png" width="100%"/>

</p>

### *Because your wallet deserves better analytics than your Spotify Wrapped.*

Velora is an AI-powered personal finance platform built to make managing money feel effortless.

Track expenses, analyse spending habits, scan receipts, automate recurring transactions and receive meaningful financial insights—all from one beautiful dashboard.

Money is stressful enough.

Your finance app shouldn't be.

---

## ✨ Highlights

- 💸 Smart Expense Tracking
- 📄 AI Receipt Scanner
- 📊 Interactive Dashboard
- 📈 Spending Analytics
- 📧 Automated Email Reports
- 🔁 Recurring Transactions
- 🔒 Secure Authentication
- ☁️ Cloud Storage

---

## 🧠 The Hardest Challenge

Building a clean architecture that could handle authentication, analytics, recurring transactions, receipt uploads and reporting without turning into spaghetti code.

---

## 💡 What I Learned

> Good software doesn't just store data.

> It helps people make better decisions.
